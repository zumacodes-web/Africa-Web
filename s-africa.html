Morocco 🇲🇦 Morocco is a beautiful country in North Africa. Its capital city is Rabat. The largest city is Casablanca. Morocco is famous for its beautiful beaches, mountains, deserts, and old buildings. It has a rich culture and a long history. The people of Morocco speak Arabic and Amazigh. French is also widely used. Moroccan food is very famous, especially couscous and tagine. Morocco is also known for its beautiful traditional clothes, colorful markets, and handmade carpets. Morocco is a wonderful country with many beautiful places and traditions.
